Blinkit Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

Project Overview

This project explores sales performance using a publicly available Blinkit dataset. I used Microsoft Power BI to analyse the data, identify sales patterns and understand how different product categories and outlet characteristics contribute to overall performance.

The aim was to turn the raw data into an interactive dashboard that makes it easier to understand what is happening across the business and where attention may be needed.

Business Objectives

The analysis focused on answering a few important business questions:

How much revenue is generated across the different outlet types?

Which product categories contribute the most to total sales?

How does fat content influence product sales?

How do outlet size and location relate to sales performance?

How do outlet establishment years relate to sales trends?

What does the overall customer rating tell us about performance?

Tools Used

Microsoft Power BI: Data visualisation and dashboard development.

Power Query: Data preparation and transformation.

DAX: Creation of measures and key performance indicators.

Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales performance across different dimensions, including:

Sales by Item Type: Compares the contribution of different product categories to total sales.

Sales by Outlet Type: Examines sales performance across grocery stores and different supermarket types.

Fat Content Analysis: Compares sales based on the fat content of products.

Outlet Size Analysis: Shows how sales are distributed across different outlet sizes.

Outlet Location Analysis: Examines sales performance across Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 locations.

Outlet Establishment Trend: Tracks sales across outlet establishment years.

Key Observations

From the dashboard, a few interesting patterns stood out:

Total sales across the dataset are approximately 1.20 million, with 8.52K items recorded.

Fruits and vegetables and snack foods are among the leading product categories by sales.

Tier 3 outlets record the highest sales among the three outlet location tiers displayed.

The outlet size analysis shows that Tier 3 outlets contribute the largest sales share, followed by Tier 2 and Tier 1.

The overall average customer rating is 3.92, providing a useful starting point for evaluating customer satisfaction.

Business Recommendations

Based on the patterns observed, here are some areas the business could explore:

Inventory Planning: Give attention to high-performing categories to ensure product availability and reduce missed sales opportunities.

Outlet Performance: Further investigate the factors contributing to the strong sales recorded by Tier 3 outlets.

Customer Experience: Review customer feedback and ratings to identify areas where the shopping experience can be improved.

Sales Strategy: Use outlet-level performance data to support better decisions around product distribution, promotions and resource allocation.
